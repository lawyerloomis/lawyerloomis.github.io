Mediation built around your family

Divorce doesn’t have to be a contest. Wherever we can, we guide families toward agreements they shape together, so decisions about your children, your home and your future are made by the people who will live with them.

That often means fewer hearings, lower costs and a calmer road for everyone involved. And when a case does need a judge, we’re ready to stand with you in court.

Prepared for what you’re carrying

Every family arrives with its own history. Our practice is built to meet people where they are.

Trauma-informed

We move at a pace you can manage, explain each step before it happens, and never ask you to relive more than your case requires.

Mental health sensitive

Anxiety, depression and grief often come with separation. We plan meetings, deadlines and parenting arrangements with that in mind, and without judgment.

Disability aware

We make room for accommodations in how we meet and communicate, and we understand how disability shapes custody, support and long-term planning.

Military retiree and veteran informed

We understand military retirement pay, Survivor Benefit Plan elections and VA benefits, and we build parenting plans that account for a life of service.
