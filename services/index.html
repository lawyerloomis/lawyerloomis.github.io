Services

Our practice centers on family law. We also help individuals and businesses along the Grand Strand with estate planning, probate, property and contract matters.

Family law

Divorce and separation

Contested and uncontested divorce, separation agreements, and the division of property and debt, including retirement accounts and military retired pay.

Child custody and visitation

Parenting plans built around your children’s routines, schools and relationships, and changes to those plans when life changes.

Alimony and spousal support

Fair support arrangements, whether you’re asking for support or may be asked to pay it.

Child support

Setting, enforcing and modifying child support under the South Carolina guidelines.

Family mediation

We prepare you for mediation and guide you through it, so you can reach agreements privately, often faster and at lower cost than a trial.

Beyond family law

Wills and durable powers of attorney

Plan ahead so your wishes are clear and the people you trust can act for you if they need to.

Probate

Guidance for families settling a loved one’s estate through the probate court.

Homeowners association disputes

Help for homeowners and associations with disputes over covenants, assessments and enforcement.

Landlord and tenant matters

Leases, evictions, deposits and disputes, for property owners and tenants.

Collections

Recovering money you’re owed, from demand letters through judgment.

Business contracts

Drafting, reviewing and resolving disputes over the agreements your business depends on.

Fees

We talk about cost openly at your free consultation, before you decide anything.
